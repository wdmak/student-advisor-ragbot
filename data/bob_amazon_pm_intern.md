# Bob — Amazon PM Intern (Summer 2025)

## Interview process
Amazon leans heavily on their Leadership Principles. I had four
back-to-back interviews in one day, each focused on 2-3 different
principles.

## Interview questions
- "Tell me about a time you had to make a decision with incomplete data."
- "Describe a project where you had to say no to a stakeholder."
- A case-style question: how would you prioritize features for a new
  product with limited engineering resources?

## How it felt
Exhausting but structured. Because every interviewer was scoring against
specific Leadership Principles, it felt less subjective than I expected —
almost like a rubric. The hardest part was having a fresh, non-repetitive
story ready for each interviewer.

## Techniques that helped
- Built a spreadsheet of 8-10 personal stories, each mapped to multiple
  Leadership Principles, so I never repeated a story across interviews.
- Practiced quantifying impact in every story ("reduced X by Y%") even for
  small class projects.
- Researched the specific team's product before the interview and asked
  questions about their current roadmap.
