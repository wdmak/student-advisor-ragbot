# Carol — Early-Stage Startup, Data Intern (Summer 2025)

## Interview process
Much less formal than big tech: one 30-minute call with the founder, then a
take-home data analysis task, then a final call to walk through my
results.

## Interview questions
- "Walk me through a dataset you've worked with and what you found
  interesting."
- The take-home: clean a messy CSV of user signups and find one actionable
  insight.
- "What tools are you comfortable with, and which do you actually enjoy
  using?"

## How it felt
Much more conversational than I expected, but also more ambiguous — there
was no clear rubric, so I wasn't sure what "success" looked like on the
take-home. I ended up presenting three findings instead of just one, which
seemed to work in my favor.

## Techniques that helped
- Over-communicated my reasoning in the take-home writeup, including dead
  ends I tried and ruled out.
- Asked the founder directly what a strong take-home submission looked
  like, before starting.
- Followed up after the final call with a short email summarizing one
  additional insight I'd noticed — showed initiative without being asked.
