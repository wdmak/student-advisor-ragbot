# Alice — Google SWE Intern (Summer 2025)

## Interview process
Three rounds: one phone screen (data structures, focused on trees and
graphs) and two virtual onsite interviews (one coding, one behavioral).

## Interview questions
- Given a list of intervals, merge the overlapping ones.
- Design a rate limiter for an API (light system design).
- "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate."

## How it felt
The phone screen felt fast — 45 minutes with barely enough time to explain
my approach out loud before coding. The onsite was less stressful than I
expected because the interviewer walked through the problem with me
instead of just watching silently.

## Techniques that helped
- Practiced explaining my thought process out loud before writing code,
  not just solving problems silently.
- Used the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for every
  behavioral answer, prepared in advance.
- Asked clarifying questions before diving into code — this alone seemed
  to change how interviewers responded.
